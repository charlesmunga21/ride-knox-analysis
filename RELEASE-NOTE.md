# Release Note

**To:** Director of Operations
**Cc:** Marketing Lead
**From:** Charles Munga Muiruri
**Date:** July 2026
**Re:** Updated Ride Knox public results page

The public results page now tells the whole story instead of stopping in the middle of
it. When the board last saw this page, it ended on a hard note: casual ridership had
collapsed after last summer's price increase. That part is still true, but it was only
half the picture. The page now shows what happened next — after the Day Pass launched
in March, non-member ridership climbed back to within a few points of where it stood
before the price increase, while members kept riding just as much as ever. That last
point matters: the recovery is new and returning riders, not current members trading
down to a cheaper option.

One honest caveat: we only have six months of Day Pass data, and spring and summer are
naturally busier seasons for riding. Part of this recovery could soften once fall and
winter arrive, and we won't know how much until then.

Where to click first: start at the top of the page. The headline and chart there give
the full verdict in one look, with links below for anyone who wants the fuller write-ups
behind the numbers.
